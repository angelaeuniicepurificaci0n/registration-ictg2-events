About the Website

What is the purpose of this website?

This website is created for school event registration to make it easier for students to view and register for school activities if they are interested to participate in the said events.


How to Register

  1. Open the Events page.
  2. Choose an event.
  3. Read its date, time, location, and requirements.
  4. Click Register.
  5. Complete the registration form.
  6. Confirm your registration.
  7. Check the Registered List.
